Cold years, witch years?

Scotland accused {{total.people|int}} people of witchcraft between 1563 and 1736, most of them in a handful of sudden panics. I mapped where they lived, counted who they were, and tested whether the panics followed cold, hard years.

Dr Tristan Hotham, Independent researcher · October 2026 · Data: Survey of Scottish Witchcraft, Wikidata, European seasonal temperature reconstructions (Luterbacher, Xoplaki and colleagues)
